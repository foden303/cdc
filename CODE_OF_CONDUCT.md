# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards

**Do:**
- Be respectful and inclusive
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the community

**Don't:**
- Harass, troll, or insult others
- Publish others' private information
- Use sexualized language or imagery
- Engage in any conduct inappropriate for a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/foden303/cdc/issues) or contacting the maintainers directly.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that violate this code, and may ban repeat offenders.
